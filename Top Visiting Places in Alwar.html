  1. Sariska Tiger Reserve

    2. Description - The lesser-known neighbor of Ranthambore, Sariska National Park is one of Rajasthan's most underrated wildlife destinations. But calling it "underrated" misses the magic. Sariska Tiger Reserve, located in Alwar district of Rajasthan, India, spans 1,203.34 km² and is renowned for its Bengal tigers, diverse wildlife, and historical landmarks. Sariska Tiger Reserve is situated in the Aravalli hills of Alwar, Rajasthan. The Sariska forest was first recognized as a Wildlife Reserve in 1955, became a Wildlife Sanctuary in 1958, and was declared a Tiger Reserve under Project Tiger in 1978. It was designated a National Park in 1982. The reserve’s vegetation includes dhok (Anogeissus pendula) as the dominant tree, along with salar, kadaya, dhak, gol, ber, khair, arjun, gugal, and bamboo.
